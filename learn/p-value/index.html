p 值、尾部与业务解释

在零假设下理解极端程度，避免倒置概率。

p 值是在 H₀ 与检验模型条件下，得到至少与观测结果同样极端的统计量的概率。极端的方向由备择决定。

右尾 z 检验为 1−Φ(z)，左尾为 Φ(z)，双尾对称正态检验为 2[1−Φ(|z|)]。不能看完结果后随意改成更小的单尾 p。

p≤α 时按规则拒绝 H₀。p 不是 H₀ 为真的概率，也不度量效应大小；统计显著不自动意味着业务上值得实施。

p(two-sided z)=2[1−Φ(|z|)]
p≤α → reject H₀; p>α → do not reject H₀
  • extreme：由备择方向定义的极端
  • α：预先选定的显著性水平

逐步推导：每一步怎么来

尾事件定义、对称性与阈值规则

  1. 固定检验统计量与预先确定的备择。p值在H₀参考分布下计算，累加至少与观测值同样极端的统计量结果，而不是给H₀赋予概率。

  2. 标准正态右尾：p=P(Z≥zobs)=1−Φ(zobs)；左尾：p=P(Z≤zobs)=Φ(zobs)，因为连续分布没有端点质量。

  3. 双侧以∣Z∣衡量极端。事件∣Z∣≥∣zobs∣分成两个不相交尾部：Z≥∣zobs∣和Z≤−∣zobs∣。

  4. 由正态对称性Φ(−a)=1−Φ(a)，两个尾部各为1−Φ(∣zobs∣)，相加得到p=2[1−Φ(∣zobs∣)]。非对称或离散检验不能不加判断套用“双倍单尾”。

  5. 在该连续精确检验中，p≤α等价于∣Z∣≥Φ⁻¹(1−α/2)，其H₀概率为α，因此得到水平α的拒绝规则。p>α表示证据不足，不是证明H₀。

计算题：从已知到答案

观察到z=2，分别求左尾、右尾、双尾p；按预先指定双尾检验、α=0.05作结论。

  1. Φ(2)≈0.977249868，因此左尾p≈0.977249868。

  2. 右尾p=1−0.977249868≈0.022750132。

  3. 双尾p=2×0.022750132≈0.045500264。

  4. 预定双尾p<0.05，拒绝H₀；三个p对应三个不同备择，不能算完后挑最小的来报告。

分步例题

双尾 z 检验观测 z=2，α=0.05。

  1. 标准正态右尾约 0.02275。
  2. 双尾 p≈2×0.02275=0.0455。
  3. 0.0455≤0.05，因此按规则拒绝 H₀。
  4. 报告差异与区间，并讨论业务意义；不是说 H₀ 有 4.55% 概率为真。
  • 不拒绝 H₀ 不是接受或证明 H₀。

p-values, tails & interpretation

Interpret extremeness under H₀ without reversing the probability.

A p-value is the probability, under H₀ and the test model, of a statistic at least as extreme as observed. The alternative determines the relevant direction.

For z tests, upper-tail p=1−Φ(z), lower-tail p=Φ(z), and two-sided p=2[1−Φ(|z|)]. Do not switch tails after seeing the result to obtain a smaller p.

Reject by the rule when p≤α. p is not the probability H₀ is true and does not measure effect size; significance does not establish practical value.

p(two-sided z)=2[1−Φ(|z|)]
p≤α → reject H₀; p>α → do not reject H₀
  • extreme: extremeness defined by the alternative
  • α: prespecified significance level

Step-by-step derivation

Tail events, symmetry and a threshold rule

  1. Fix the statistic and prespecified alternative. A p-value sums reference-null outcomes at least as extreme as observed; it does not assign a probability to H₀.

  2. For standard normal tests, right-tail p=1−Φ(zobs) and left-tail p=Φ(zobs); endpoint equality has zero mass.

  3. For two sides, ∣Z∣≥∣zobs∣ splits into the disjoint upper and lower tails.

  4. Normal symmetry makes both tails equal to 1−Φ(∣zobs∣), giving twice that value. Do not blindly double a one-tail p for asymmetric or discrete tests.

  5. Here p≤α is equivalent to crossing the two-sided critical value, an event with null probability α. Nonrejection means insufficient evidence, not proof of H₀.

Calculation problem and worked solution

For z=2 calculate left-, right- and two-tail p-values. Make the decision for a prespecified two-sided test at 0.05.

  1. The left-tail p is about 0.977249868.

  2. Right-tail p=1−0.977249868≈0.022750132.

  3. Two-sided p=2×0.022750132≈0.045500264.

  4. The prespecified two-sided test rejects. These three values correspond to different alternatives; do not select the smallest after seeing data.

Worked example

A two-sided z test observes z=2 with α=0.05.

  1. The standard normal upper tail is about 0.02275.
  2. The two-sided p is about 0.0455.
  3. Reject H₀ because 0.0455≤0.05.
  4. Report the difference, interval and practical relevance; this is not a 4.55% probability that H₀ is true.
  • Not rejecting H₀ is not accepting or proving it.
