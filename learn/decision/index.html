先定义决策问题

分清能选择的行动与不能控制的随机结果。

DMD 把管理问题连接到数据、模型与决策。先说明谁在什么时候做什么选择，再列出影响收益的未知因素。模型是带假设的简化，不是现实的复制。

行动由决策者选择，市场强弱等状态由不确定环境决定。收益需要统一单位、时间范围和成本口径，不能把收入与净利润混在一起比较。

信息在什么时候到达会影响可行策略。不能先看未来结果，再假装早先就能做出相应选择。选择期望收益最大还意味着采用特定的风险评价准则。

问题 / Question → 数据 / Data → 假设 / Assumptions → 模型 / Model → 决策 / Decision

  • state：不能直接选择的环境状态

逐步推导：每一步怎么来

建模流程与期望值决策准则

  1. “问题→数据→假设→模型→决策”是建模流程，不是可以代数证明的等式。先定义可选行动a和不可控状态s。

  2. 用v(a,s)记录同单位收益，用p(s)表示状态概率，要求p(s)≥0且Σp(s)=1。

  3. 同一行动在各状态下的平均收益按概率加权：EMV(a)=Σp(s)v(a,s)。这是期望的定义。

  4. 若目标就是最大化期望货币收益、所有方案可行且不另计风险厌恶，则选择argmaxₐ EMV(a)。若目标换成效用或成本，准则也必须随之改变。

计算题：从已知到答案

保底项目收益40；新项目以0.5概率赚100，以0.5概率亏20。按期望收益比较，并量化新项目波动。

  1. 保底EMV=40；新项目EMV=0.5×100+0.5×(−20)=50−10=40。

  2. 新项目相对均值偏差为100−40=60和−20−40=−60。

  3. 其方差=0.5×60²+0.5×(−60)²=3600，标准差60；保底标准差0。

  4. 仅按期望值两者并列，不能声称其中一个一定更好；风险准则需要另行说明。

分步例题

选择稳妥项目收益 40，或新项目：成功收益 100，失败收益 −20。成功概率 0.5。

  1. 选择对象是项目，成功与失败是状态。
  2. 统一收益单位，并把失败损失写成负数。
  3. 新项目期望为 0.5×100+0.5×(−20)=40。
  4. 按期望值两者相同，但收益波动不同，需要讨论风险偏好。
  • 期望相同不等于两个方案对每个人都同样好。

Define a decision problem

Distinguish choices from uncertain outcomes.

DMD connects management questions to data, models and decisions. Identify who chooses what and when, then list uncertain factors affecting outcomes. A model is a simplification with assumptions.

The decision maker chooses an action; uncertain states such as demand are not controllable. Compare payoffs on a consistent basis of units, time and costs, distinguishing revenue from net profit.

Information timing determines feasible strategies. You cannot observe a future outcome and pretend it was available earlier. Maximizing expected payoff also specifies a risk criterion.

Question → Data → Assumptions → Model → Decision
  • action: an available choice
  • state: an uncertain environmental condition

Step-by-step derivation

A modeling workflow and an expected-payoff decision rule

  1. “Question → data → assumptions → model → decision” is a workflow, not an algebraic identity. Define actions a and uncontrollable states s.

  2. Record payoffs v(a,s) in consistent units and state probabilities p(s)≥0 summing to 1.

  3. The probability-weighted payoff is EMV(a)=Σp(s)v(a,s), by the definition of expectation.

  4. If the objective is expected monetary payoff, all actions are feasible and no separate risk preference is modeled, choose argmaxₐ EMV(a). Utility or cost objectives require a different criterion.

Calculation problem and worked solution

A safe project pays 40. A new project pays 100 or −20 with equal probability. Compare expected payoffs and quantify the new project's variability.

  1. Safe EMV=40. New EMV=0.5×100+0.5×(−20)=50−10=40.

  2. The new project's deviations from its mean are 60 and −60.

  3. New variance=0.5×60²+0.5×(−60)²=3600, so SD=√3600=60; safe SD=0.

  4. The expected-value criterion gives a tie. A preference requires an additional risk criterion.

Worked example

Choose a safe payoff of 40 or a new project paying 100 on success and −20 on failure, with success probability 0.5.

  1. Projects are actions; success and failure are states.
  2. Use consistent payoff units and write a loss as negative.
  3. The new project has expected payoff 40.
  4. Expected payoffs tie, but the risk profiles differ.
  • Equal expected payoffs do not imply equal appeal to every decision maker.
