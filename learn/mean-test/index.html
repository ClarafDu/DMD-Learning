单样本均值检验

把均值差换成标准误单位，再按方向计算证据。

检验统计量用“样本均值减基准均值”除以标准误。σ 已知时用 z；σ 未知时用 s 估计并使用合适的 t 方法。

独立正态样本下 t 检验有精确依据，非正态小样本要评估形状和异常值，可能需要其他方法；大样本近似也不是无条件保证。

算完统计量还要配合尾部方向与参考分布。相同的 t 值，在不同自由度和单尾、双尾下会得到不同 p 值。

z=(x̄−μ₀)/(σ/√n)
t=(x̄−μ₀)/(s/√n), df=n−1
  • μ₀：零假设给定的均值
  • t：σ 未知时的标准化统计量

逐步推导：每一步怎么来

在原假设下中心化与t比值

  1. 在H₀边界μ=μ₀下，独立正态样本均值X̄~N(μ₀,σ²/n)。从均值减去μ₀，再除以其正标准差σ/√n，得到z统计量。

  2. 因此Z=(X̄−μ₀)/(σ/√n)~N(0,1)。分母是样本均值的标准差，不能用个体σ直接替代。

  3. σ未知时，由正态样本的正交分解，V=(n−1)S²/σ²是n−1个独立标准正态平方和，且与Z独立；均值区间的推导中给出了坐标分解过程。

  4. 按t分布定义，T=Z/√[V/(n−1)]。代入并约去σ，得到T=(X̄−μ₀)/(S/√n)，自由度n−1。

  5. 观测值z或t只是横轴位置。p值需要按预定备择方向在相应参考分布上求尾面积；非正态时必须另行论证近似有效性。

计算题：从已知到答案

n=25、x̄=52、s=10，独立正态样本。双侧检验μ₀=50，α=0.05；再比较σ已知为10的z统计量。

  1. σ未知用t：SE=10/√25=2，df=24。

  2. t=(52−50)/2=1。

  3. 双侧临界值约±2.063898562，∣1∣较小，所以不拒绝；数值t尾积分给p≈0.32728688。

  4. 若σ已知为10，z也等于1，但双侧标准正态p≈0.31731051；同一统计量数值并不意味着同一参考分布或p值。

分步例题

独立正态样本，n=25、x̄=52、s=10，检验 μ₀=50。

  1. σ 未知，用 t，df=24。
  2. 估计 SE=10/5=2。
  3. t=(52−50)/2=1。
  4. 再按预先确定的方向求 t 尾部概率，不能直接把 t=1 当作 p=1。
  • 统计量是无单位的距离，不是概率。

One-sample mean tests

Express a mean difference in SE units and evaluate it in the chosen direction.

A test statistic divides sample mean minus null mean by an SE. Use z with known σ and a suitable t method with estimated s when σ is unknown.

The t test is exact for independent normal samples. Small nonnormal samples need assessment of shape and outliers and may require other methods; large-sample approximation is not unconditional either.

The statistic must be paired with a tail and reference distribution. The same t value has different p-values for different degrees of freedom or tail choices.

z=(x̄−μ₀)/(σ/√n)
t=(x̄−μ₀)/(s/√n), df=n−1
  • μ₀: mean specified by H₀
  • t: standardized statistic using estimated SD

Step-by-step derivation

Centering under the null and forming a t ratio

  1. At the null boundary, X̄~N(μ₀,σ²/n) under iid normal sampling. Centering and scaling yields the z statistic.

  2. Thus Z=(X̄−μ₀)/(σ/√n) is standard normal. The denominator is the mean's SD, not the individual σ.

  3. For unknown σ, the normal-sample orthogonal decomposition gives V=(n−1)S²/σ²~χ²ₙ₋₁ independent of Z; the mean-CI derivation shows this decomposition.

  4. By the t definition, T=Z/√[V/(n−1)]. Substitution cancels σ, yielding T=(X̄−μ₀)/(S/√n) with n−1 degrees of freedom.

  5. An observed z or t is a location, not a p-value. Compute the prespecified tail in its reference distribution; nonnormal sampling requires a justified approximation.

Calculation problem and worked solution

For iid normal n=25, x̄=52 and s=10, test μ₀=50 two-sided at 0.05. Compare the z statistic if σ is known to be 10.

  1. Use t for unknown σ: SE=10/√25=2 and df=25−1=24.

  2. t=(52−50)/2=1.

  3. The two-sided critical values are about ±2.063898562, so do not reject. Numerical t-tail integration gives p≈0.32728688.

  4. With known σ=10, z is also 1, but the normal two-sided p≈0.31731051. Equal statistic values do not imply equal p-values.

Worked example

An independent normal sample has n=25, x̄=52 and s=10. Test μ₀=50.

  1. Use t with df=24 because σ is unknown.
  2. Estimated SE=2.
  3. t=1.
  4. Compute the t tail probability in the prespecified direction; t=1 is not p=1.
  • A statistic is a dimensionless distance, not a probability.
