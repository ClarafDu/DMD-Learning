正态分布：中心与宽度

用 μ 与 σ 解释钟形曲线，先判断建模条件。

正态分布以均值 μ 为对称中心，标准差 σ>0 决定宽窄。改变 μ 平移曲线，增大 σ 会让曲线变宽、峰值变低，总面积仍为 1。

正态模型下，大约 68.27% 的概率在 μ±σ，95.45% 在 μ±2σ。这些是模型性质，不能不加检查地套到所有数据。

本站统一写 N(μ,σ²)，第二个参数是方差。课件有些地方用第二个参数表示标准差；比较时先读清约定，本站实验会显式显示 SD。

X~N(μ,σ²), σ>0
P(μ−σ≤X≤μ+σ) ≈ 0.6827
  • μ：曲线中心，也就是均值
  • σ：标准差，控制分散程度

逐步推导：每一步怎么来

分布定义、正态化与区间面积

  1. 正态是一种模型，不是所有数据的必然结论。标准正态密度设为φ(z)=c exp(−z²/2)。令I=∫₋∞∞ exp(−z²/2)dz。

  2. I²是平面上exp[−(x²+y²)/2]的积分。换极坐标，面积元dxdy=r dr dθ，得到I²=∫₀²πdθ ∫₀∞e^(−r²/2)r dr=2π×1，所以I=√(2π)，c=1/√(2π)。

  3. φ关于0对称，所以E(Z)=0。又φ′(z)=−zφ(z)，分部积分给E(Z²)=∫z²φ(z)dz=[−zφ(z)]₋∞∞+∫φ(z)dz=1，所以Var(Z)=1。

  4. 令X=μ+σZ，σ>0，则E(X)=μ、Var(X)=σ²。换元z=(x−μ)/σ、dz=dx/σ，得到fX(x)=φ((x−μ)/σ)/σ；记作N(μ,σ²)。

  5. μ−σ≤X≤μ+σ等价于−1≤Z≤1。因此概率=Φ(1)−Φ(−1)=2Φ(1)−1。Φ没有初等原函数，用数值积分得到Φ(1)≈0.841344746，概率≈0.682689492。

计算题：从已知到答案

用时X~N(60,100)分钟，求50至70分钟内完成的概率；1000次独立任务中预期多少次在此范围？

  1. σ=√100=10，下界z=(50−60)/10=−1，上界z=(70−60)/10=1。

  2. 查或数值计算Φ(1)≈0.841344746，Φ(−1)≈0.158655254。

  3. 区间概率相减=0.682689492≈68.27%。

  4. 预期次数=1000×0.682689492≈682.69次；实际次数是随机整数，不保证等于683。

分步例题

考试用时建模为正态，均值 60 分钟、标准差 10 分钟。

  1. 中心位置在 60。
  2. 50 到 70 是 μ±σ。
  3. 该区间概率约 68.27%。
  4. 是否适合用正态还需检查形状、异常值和不可能出现的负值范围。
  • 看到均值与标准差，不代表数据自动服从正态。

Normal distribution: center & width

Use μ and σ to interpret a bell curve and consider model suitability.

A normal distribution is symmetric about μ and has spread σ>0. Changing μ shifts it; increasing σ widens it and lowers its peak while preserving area 1.

Under a normal model, about 68.27% lies within μ±σ and 95.45% within μ±2σ. These are model properties, not universal data facts.

This site uses N(μ,σ²), with variance as the second parameter. Some slides use SD as the second parameter. Read the convention and check the explicitly labeled SD in the experiments.

X~N(μ,σ²), σ>0
P(μ−σ≤X≤μ+σ) ≈ 0.6827
  • μ: center and mean
  • σ: SD, controlling spread

Step-by-step derivation

Distribution definition, normalization and interval area

  1. A normal distribution is a model, not a consequence for all data. Set φ(z)=c exp(−z²/2) and I=∫₋∞∞ exp(−z²/2)dz.

  2. Square I and use polar coordinates: I²=∫₀²πdθ∫₀∞exp(−r²/2)r dr=2π, so c=1/√(2π).

  3. Symmetry gives E(Z)=0. Since φ′(z)=−zφ(z), integration by parts gives E(Z²)=[−zφ(z)]₋∞∞+∫φ(z)dz=1, so Var(Z)=1.

  4. For X=μ+σZ with σ>0, its mean is μ and variance σ². Change of variables gives fX(x)=φ((x−μ)/σ)/σ, denoted N(μ,σ²).

  5. The interval μ±σ maps to −1≤Z≤1. Its probability is 2Φ(1)−1≈0.682689492, using numerical integration because Φ has no elementary antiderivative.

Calculation problem and worked solution

Completion time is N(60,100) minutes. Find P(50≤X≤70) and the expected count among 1000 independent tasks.

  1. σ=√100=10. The standardized limits are (50−60)/10=−1 and (70−60)/10=1.

  2. The CDF values are approximately 0.841344746 and 0.158655254.

  3. Subtract: 0.841344746−0.158655254=0.682689492, or approximately 68.27%.

  4. Expected count ≈682.69; an actual count is a random integer, not guaranteed to equal 683.

Worked example

Completion time is modeled as normal with mean 60 minutes and SD 10 minutes.

  1. The center is 60.
  2. 50 to 70 is μ±σ.
  3. The interval probability is about 68.27%.
  4. Check data shape, outliers and the relevance of impossible negative times before accepting the model.
  • Knowing a mean and SD does not establish normality.
