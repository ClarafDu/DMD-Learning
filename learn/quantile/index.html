分位数与服务水平

从给定概率反求阈值，并区分覆盖个体与估计均值。

概率计算是已知 x 求 F(x)；分位数是给定 q，反求使 F(x)=q 的阈值。正态情况下，先求标准正态分位数 z，再换回原单位。

例如库存覆盖需求的 95%，要找需求分布的第 95 百分位。若商品必须为整数且要求不低于服务水平，需要向上取整并检查实际条件。

覆盖单个未来需求的区间与总体均值的置信区间不同：前者反映个体波动，后者反映估计均值的不确定性。

xq = μ + zqσ;  Φ(zq)=q
Excel: NORM.INV(q,mean,sd)
  • q：左侧累计概率
  • xq：对应的原单位阈值

逐步推导：每一步怎么来

反解累积概率与线性还原

  1. 正态q分位数xq定义为P(X≤xq)=q，0<q<1且σ>0。标准化后Φ((xq−μ)/σ)=q。

  2. 标准正态CDF连续严格递增，存在唯一逆值zq=Φ⁻¹(q)，即Φ(zq)=q。

  3. 由(xq−μ)/σ=zq，两边乘σ、再加μ，得xq=μ+σzq。

  4. NORM.INV(q,mean,sd)是在计算这个逆函数；第三参数是标准差而不是方差。逆CDF通常用数值算法，不是靠初等代数直接求出zq。

计算题：从已知到答案

需求X~N(100,100)，求95%分位点与整数库存配置。

  1. μ=100，σ=10，q=0.95；数值逆CDF给zq≈1.644853627。

  2. xq=100+10×1.644853627=116.44853627。

  3. 若库存必须为整数且希望该连续近似下左侧概率至少95%，向上取整为117。

  4. 代回117：z=1.7，Φ(1.7)≈0.955435，大于0.95；整数取整与模型近似应一并说明。

分步例题

需求正态，μ=100、σ=10，95% 分位的 z≈1.645。

  1. 目标写成 P(X≤s)=0.95。
  2. 标准正态阈值约 1.645。
  3. s=100+1.645×10=116.45。
  4. 若按整数单位配置，选 117 并注明正态近似。
  • 95% 单侧分位数约 1.645；95% 双侧中心区间使用约 ±1.96。

Quantiles & service levels

Find a threshold from a probability and distinguish individual coverage from mean estimation.

A CDF maps x to probability; a quantile maps probability q back to a threshold. For a normal model, find the standard normal quantile and convert back to original units.

To cover demand with 95% probability, use the 95th percentile of the demand distribution. For integer stock and an at-least requirement, round upward and check the service condition.

An interval covering individual future demand differs from a confidence interval for the population mean: individual spread versus uncertainty in estimating the mean.

xq = μ + zqσ;  Φ(zq)=q
Excel: NORM.INV(q,mean,sd)
  • q: cumulative probability to the left
  • xq: threshold in original units

Step-by-step derivation

Inverting a CDF and reversing standardization

  1. For 0<q<1, the normal quantile satisfies P(X≤xq)=q, hence Φ((xq−μ)/σ)=q.

  2. The standard-normal CDF is continuous and strictly increasing, giving a unique zq=Φ⁻¹(q).

  3. Solve (xq−μ)/σ=zq by multiplying by σ and adding μ to obtain xq=μ+σzq.

  4. NORM.INV(q,mean,sd) evaluates this inverse numerically. Its third argument is SD, not variance; zq is not generally available by elementary algebra.

Calculation problem and worked solution

Demand is N(100,100). Find the 95th percentile and an integer stock level.

  1. The inputs are μ=100, σ=10, q=0.95, with zq≈1.644853627.

  2. The percentile is 116.44853627.

  3. For integer stock with at least 95% left probability under this continuous model, round up to 117.

  4. At 117, z=1.7 and Φ(1.7)≈0.955435>0.95. State both rounding and the modeling approximation.

Worked example

Demand is normal with μ=100 and σ=10; the 95% z quantile is about 1.645.

  1. Write P(X≤s)=0.95.
  2. The standard normal threshold is about 1.645.
  3. s=116.45.
  4. For integer stock, choose 117 and state the normal approximation.
  • The one-sided 95% quantile is about 1.645; a central 95% interval uses about ±1.96.
