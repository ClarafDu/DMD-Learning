含截距且设计矩阵满秩时，估计 k+1 个系数后还有 n−k−1 个独立残差方向。与简单回归同理，每个方向贡献 σ² 的期望平方。

  • ŷ：拟合值；y：观测值；SSE：残差平方和。

线性回归逐步推导

  1. n 为观测数，k 为解释变量个数。模型同时使用多个变量预测平均结果。

  2. 只改变 xⱼ，把两次拟合值相减，其余项抵消，得到 Δŷ=bⱼΔxⱼ。

  3. 含截距且设计矩阵满秩时，估计 k+1 个系数后还有 n−k−1 个独立残差方向。与简单回归同理，每个方向贡献 σ² 的期望平方。

  4. 写成所需形式：ŷ=b₀+Σbⱼxⱼ；s²=SSE/(n−k−1)

计算题：从已知到答案

预测工单量为 ŷ=30+4x₁−2x₂。若 x₁=5、x₂=3，求预测；x₂ 再增 1 而 x₁ 不变，预测如何变化？

  1. 确定所求量，读清各变量的单位。

  2. 只改变 xⱼ，把两次拟合值相减，其余项抵消，得到 Δŷ=bⱼΔxⱼ。

  3. 代入得 30+20−6=44；增加 x₂ 后预测减少 2，变为 42。

  4. 用业务单位解释结果，留意模型适用范围。

Multiple linear regression and prediction

n counts observations and k counts predictors. The model predicts mean response from several predictors.

n counts observations and k counts predictors. The model predicts mean response from several predictors.

Holding other predictors fixed and subtracting fitted values gives Δŷ=bⱼΔxⱼ.

With an intercept and full column rank, k+1 fitted coefficients leave n−k−1 residual directions. Each contributes expected squared error σ² as in simple regression.

ŷ=b₀+Σbⱼxⱼ; s²=SSE/(n−k−1)
  • ŷ: fitted value; y: observation; SSE: residual sum of squares.
  • b₀ is the intercept, bⱼ a slope; Sxx and Sxy are centered sums of squares and cross-products.

Step-by-step derivation

Linear regression derivation

  1. n counts observations and k counts predictors. The model predicts mean response from several predictors.

  3. With an intercept and full column rank, k+1 fitted coefficients leave n−k−1 residual directions. Each contributes expected squared error σ² as in simple regression.

  4. Collect the terms: ŷ=b₀+Σbⱼxⱼ; s²=SSE/(n−k−1)

Calculation problem and worked solution

Order count is predicted by ŷ=30+4x₁−2x₂. Predict at x₁=5 and x₂=3, then increase x₂ by one with x₁ fixed.

  1. Identify the requested quantity and its units.

  2. Holding other predictors fixed and subtracting fitted values gives Δŷ=bⱼΔxⱼ.

  3. Substitution gives 30+20−6=44. Increasing x₂ lowers the prediction by 2 to 42.

  4. Interpret in business units and check the model’s scope.
