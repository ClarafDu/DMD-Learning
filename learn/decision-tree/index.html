决策树与逆向计算

按时间画树，从末端向前计算。

方形节点表示选择，圆形节点表示随机结果。每个随机节点的分支概率要加起来等于 1，末端写清完整收益。

逆向计算时，随机节点取概率加权平均；决策节点按准则选择较好的分支。先解决末端决策，再逐层退回根节点。

若调查有费用，要在所有相关路径计入；若调查结果会影响行动，需要允许结果出现之后选择不同方案。条件概率与信息时序必须一致。

敏感性分析把成功概率、收益或成本当作可变输入，检查最优策略何时改变。先求两个方案期望相等的临界值，再检验临界值两侧；本站决策树实验可直接观察这一过程。

机会节点 / Chance: EV = Σ pᵢvᵢ
决策节点 / Decision: choose the preferred feasible branch
  • pᵢ：分支 i 的条件概率
  • vᵢ：该分支的收益

逐步推导：每一步怎么来

全期望与逆向归纳

  1. 在机会节点，设后续分支i以条件概率pᵢ发生，终点收益vᵢ已包含后续成本，Σpᵢ=1。

  2. 将同一分支下的终点先求期望，再乘该分支概率后相加，等于逐个终点直接加权。这是分配律与全期望：E(V)=ΣP(i)E(V∣i)。

  3. 若一个分支已到终点，其条件期望就是vᵢ，所以节点值EV=Σpᵢvᵢ。

  4. 在决策节点，分支是自己可选的行动，不是随机抽签。最大期望收益准则下取max，最小期望成本准则下取min；不能把选项收益无条件平均。

  5. 从末端向前，用已求出的子树最优值替换子树。每一步比较都在同一个已知信息集合下进行，有限树重复此操作即可得到根节点最优值。

计算题：从已知到答案

保底收益30；冒险成功概率0.6，成功收益80，失败收益−20。求机会节点值并作决策。

  1. 失败概率=1−0.6=0.4，两个分支概率之和为1。

  2. 成功加权收益=0.6×80=48；失败加权收益=0.4×(−20)=−8。

  3. 冒险机会节点EV=48−8=40。

  4. 决策节点max(30,40)=40，按该准则选冒险，期望优势40−30=10。

分步例题

保底收益 30，冒险方案成功概率 0.6，成功 80、失败 −20。

  1. 在起点画选择节点：保底或冒险。
  2. 在冒险分支后画机会节点：0.6 成功、0.4 失败。
  3. 逆推冒险收益：0.6×80+0.4×(−20)=40。
  4. 按最大期望收益准则选冒险，40 比 30 高 10。
  • 在随机节点不能把较差结果删掉；那不是你能选择的结果。

Decision trees & rollback

Draw a chronological tree and evaluate it from right to left.

A square node represents a choice and a circle an uncertain outcome. Probabilities leaving each chance node sum to 1; terminal nodes show complete payoffs.

In rollback, average payoffs at chance nodes using probabilities, and choose the preferred branch at decision nodes. Evaluate later choices before earlier ones.

Include information costs on every relevant path. If survey results guide action, allow different actions after different results. Conditional probabilities must agree with information timing.

Sensitivity analysis varies a probability, payoff or cost to find when the preferred strategy changes. Solve for equal expected payoffs, then inspect both sides of the break-even value; the decision-tree experiment illustrates this.

Chance: EV = Σ pᵢvᵢ
Decision: choose the preferred feasible branch
  • pᵢ: conditional probability of branch i
  • vᵢ: payoff on that branch

Step-by-step derivation

Total expectation and backward induction

  1. At a chance node, branch i occurs with conditional probability pᵢ and has payoff vᵢ including subsequent costs; the probabilities sum to 1.

  2. Averaging within each branch and then weighting branches equals directly weighting all leaves: E(V)=ΣP(i)E(V∣i), the law of total expectation.

  3. At a terminal branch, the conditional expectation is vᵢ, giving EV=Σpᵢvᵢ.

  4. At a decision node branches are choices, not a random lottery. Maximize expected payoff or minimize expected cost; do not average the actions without a randomization rule.

  5. Work backward, replacing each subtree by its optimal value under the information then available. Repeating on a finite tree yields the optimal root value.

Calculation problem and worked solution

A safe action pays 30. A risky action pays 80 with probability 0.6 and −20 otherwise. Evaluate the chance node and choose.

  1. Failure probability is 0.4; the branch probabilities sum to 1.

  2. Success contributes 0.6×80=48; failure contributes 0.4×(−20)=−8.

  3. The risky chance node has EV=48−8=40.

  4. Choose max(30,40)=40. The risky action has expected advantage 10 under this criterion.

Worked example

A safe option pays 30; a risky option pays 80 with probability 0.6 and −20 otherwise.

  1. Draw a decision node for safe versus risky.
  2. After risky, draw chance branches with probabilities 0.6 and 0.4.
  3. Roll back the risky option: expected payoff 40.
  4. Under expected-payoff maximization choose risky; its advantage is 10.
  • Do not discard an unfavorable chance outcome; it is not your choice.
