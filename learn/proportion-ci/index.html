总体比例的区间估计

区分比例、计数和比例标准误，并检查近似条件。

二分类数据中，用成功次数除以总次数得到样本比例 p̂。这里成功仅是所关注类别，如赞成或退货，比例没有原始货币单位。

课件入门 Wald 区间使用 p̂±z√[p̂(1−p̂)/n]。它需要合适的随机样本、独立性与足够的两类计数；小样本或靠近 0、1 时可能表现不好。

区间超出 [0,1] 是近似局限的信号，不能只机械截断就当作可靠覆盖。实际应用可考虑 Wilson 等方法，本节明确保留课程的入门近似。

p̂=x/n
SE≈√[p̂(1−p̂)/n]; CI≈p̂±z×SE
  • p̂：p hat，样本比例
  • x：所关注类别的计数

逐步推导：每一步怎么来

伯努利均值与Wald近似区间

  1. 把每个回答写成独立Bernoulli(p)变量Iᵢ：赞成为1，否则0。成功数x是ΣIᵢ的观测值，样本比例p̂=x/n就是这些0、1的样本均值。

  2. E(Iᵢ)=p，Var(Iᵢ)=p(1−p)，所以E(p̂)=p，Var(p̂)=p(1−p)/n，真实标准误为√[p(1−p)/n]。

  3. p未知，Wald方法代入p̂估计标准误：SEhat=√[p̂(1−p̂)/n]。大样本下标准化比例近似标准正态。

  4. 把估计标准误视为本次样本已计算出的量，从−z*≤(p̂−p)/SEhat≤z*反解，得到p̂±z*SEhat。代入与正态化都引入近似，不是精确覆盖。

  5. 要检查独立随机抽样及足够成功、失败数。本页采用各至少10的入门检查；靠近0或1时Wald区间可能很差甚至越界，应改用Wilson或精确法，不能仅截断就声称修复覆盖。

计算题：从已知到答案

随机独立调查100人，60人赞成。求95% Wald比例区间。

  1. p̂=60/100=0.6，成功60、失败40，通过本例计数检查。

  2. 估计方差=0.6×0.4/100=0.0024，SEhat=√0.0024≈0.048989795。

  3. 误差界=1.959963985×0.048989795≈0.096018234。

  4. 区间≈[0.503981766,0.696018234]，即[50.40%,69.60%]；这是总体赞成比例的近似区间。

分步例题

随机独立调查 100 人，60 人赞成，构造近似 95% 比例区间。

  1. p̂=60/100=0.6。
  2. 两类计数为 60 与 40，足以用于本例近似。
  3. SE=√(0.6×0.4/100)≈0.049。
  4. 区间约 0.6±1.96×0.049，即 [0.504,0.696]。
  • 比例区间的标准误不能直接用均值题中的 s。

Confidence intervals for proportions

Distinguish counts, proportions and SEs, checking approximation conditions.

For binary data, p̂ is successes divided by trials. Success just labels the target category, such as approval or return, and a proportion is dimensionless.

The introductory Wald interval is p̂±z√[p̂(1−p̂)/n]. It needs suitable random sampling, independence and adequate counts of both outcomes. It can perform poorly for small samples or proportions near 0 or 1.

An interval outside [0,1] signals limitations; clipping it does not establish good coverage. Other methods such as Wilson may be preferable in applications; this lesson identifies the course’s introductory approximation.

p̂=x/n
SE≈√[p̂(1−p̂)/n]; CI≈p̂±z×SE
  • p̂: p hat, sample proportion
  • x: count in the target category

Step-by-step derivation

A Bernoulli mean and the Wald approximation

  1. Encode iid responses as Bernoulli(p) indicators. The observed count is x and p̂=x/n is their sample mean.

  2. Thus E(p̂)=p and Var(p̂)=p(1−p)/n, giving true SE √[p(1−p)/n].

  3. The Wald method estimates the unknown p by p̂ in the SE. Under a suitable large-sample approximation the standardized proportion is approximately normal.

  4. Treat the computed SE as positive and invert −z*≤(p̂−p)/SEhat≤z* to get p̂±z*SEhat. Both plug-in estimation and normal approximation make coverage approximate.

  5. Check independent random sampling and adequate successes and failures, using 10 each here as a basic check. Near 0 or 1 the Wald interval can fail; consider Wilson or exact methods rather than claiming clipping fixes coverage.

Calculation problem and worked solution

In an independent random survey of 100 people, 60 approve. Find a 95% Wald interval.

  1. p̂=60/100=0.6, with 60 successes and 40 failures.

  2. Estimated variance=0.6×0.4/100=0.0024; SE=√0.0024≈0.048989795.

  3. Margin=1.959963985×0.048989795≈0.096018234.

  4. The interval is [0.6−0.096018234,0.6+0.096018234]≈[0.503981766,0.696018234], or [50.40%,69.60%].

Worked example

In a random independent survey of 100 people, 60 approve. Construct an approximate 95% proportion CI.

  1. p̂=0.6.
  2. The counts 60 and 40 are adequate for this example.
  3. SE≈0.049.
  4. The interval is about [0.504,0.696].
  • Do not substitute an unrelated sample SD into a proportion interval.
