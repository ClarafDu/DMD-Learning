A trial with two outcomes, success and failure. “Success” is a chosen label, not necessarily a desirable outcome.

固定n次独立、成功概率相同的试验中，成功次数的分布。 先检查固定次数、独立、两类结果、同一p。

The count of successes in n independent trials with the same success probability. Check fixed n, independence, two outcomes and constant p.

从n个对象中选k个，不考虑顺序的选法数。 选A再选B与选B再选A算同一种。

The number of ways to choose k of n items without regard to order. Choosing A then B and choosing B then A count as one selection.

把线性共同变化程度标准化到−1到1。 相关不等于因果；零相关也不必然独立。

A standardized measure of linear association from −1 to 1. Correlation is not causation; zero correlation does not generally imply independence.

用连续区间上的密度来描述取值的变量。 单个精确点的概率为0，区间概率看面积。

A variable described by a density over a continuous range. An exact point has probability zero; interval probabilities are areas.

曲线的高度；曲线下区间面积才是概率。 密度可以大于1，但总面积必须为1。

The height of a density curve; an area under it gives probability. A density may exceed 1, but its total area must equal 1.

在范围内，相同长度的区间具有相同概率。 密度为1/(b−a)，不是每个点都有正概率。

Equal-length intervals within the range have equal probabilities. The density is 1/(b−a); individual points do not have positive probability.

由均值和方差确定的对称钟形分布。 本站第二个参数是方差σ²，不是标准差σ。

A symmetric bell-shaped distribution determined by its mean and variance. Here the second parameter is variance σ², not standard deviation σ.

把一个数改写为距离均值多少个标准差。 先减均值，再除标准差；z没有单位。

Expresses how many standard deviations a value lies from the mean. Subtract the mean, then divide by SD; z is unitless.

A cutoff corresponding to a specified cumulative probability. Use the CDF for value → probability and its inverse for probability → value.

A normal model for a collection of variables jointly. Normal marginals alone do not ensure joint normality; under joint normality, zero correlation implies independence.

满足相应条件、样本量足够大时，样本均值的分布近似正态。 变得近似正态的是均值的抽样分布，不是原始数据。

Under suitable conditions, sample means become approximately normal as sample size grows. It is the sampling distribution of the mean, not the raw data, that becomes approximately normal.

用连续分布近似整数计数时调整区间边界。 例如X≤10近似为连续变量≤10.5。

Adjusts boundaries when a continuous distribution approximates integer counts. For example, approximate X≤10 using a continuous cutoff of 10.5.

研究问题中希望了解的全部对象。 总体不是已经拿到的那一小份数据。

The entire collection the study aims to understand. The population is broader than the observed sample.

从总体中实际观测的一部分对象。 样本大不自动代表抽样没有偏差。

The subset of a population actually observed. A large sample does not automatically remove selection bias.

描述总体特征、通常未知的数值。 参数属于总体；统计量由样本算出。

A numerical characteristic of a population, usually unknown. Parameters describe populations; statistics are calculated from samples.

统计量Statisticx̄, s, p̂

只由样本数据计算出来的数值。 换一份样本，统计量可能改变。

A quantity calculated from sample data. A different sample may give a different statistic.

用一个数来估计未知总体参数。 点估计没有直接展示估计的不确定程度。

A single value used to estimate an unknown population parameter. A point estimate alone does not show estimation uncertainty.

重复按同样方法抽样时，统计量形成的分布。 区分一份样本的数据分布与多次抽样的统计量分布。

The distribution of a statistic across repeated samples taken the same way. Distinguish the values within one sample from a statistic across repeated samples.

抽样分布与标准误 · Sampling distributions & standard error
标准误Standard errorSE, σ/√n

统计量抽样分布的标准差，反映估计的波动。 对独立同分布样本的均值，SE=σ/√n；σ未知时用s估计。

The SD of a statistic's sampling distribution, describing estimation variability. For an iid sample mean, SE=σ/√n; estimate σ with s when unknown.

长期重复抽样时，构造方法覆盖真实参数的比例。 95%描述方法，不是已算区间中固定参数的随机概率。

The long-run coverage rate of an interval construction procedure. 95% describes the procedure, not a random probability for a fixed parameter in the computed interval.

置信区间Confidence intervalCI

用下限到上限表达对总体参数的估计。 结合置信水平、样本设计和模型条件一起解释。

An interval estimate for a population parameter. Interpret it with its confidence level, sampling design and model assumptions.

按指定尾部概率划定的分布分界值。 双侧95%标准正态临界值约为±1.96。

A distribution cutoff chosen for specified tail probabilities. For a two-sided 95% standard-normal interval, the cutoffs are about ±1.96.

误差界Margin of errorME

区间从点估计向两侧延伸的距离。 通常ME=临界值×标准误；它是半宽，不是全宽。

The distance an interval extends on either side of an estimate. Usually ME=critical value×SE; it is a half-width, not the full width.

样本中符合条件的个数除以样本总数。 p̂来自样本，p表示总体比例。

The count meeting a condition divided by the sample size. p̂ describes the sample; p denotes the population proportion.

样本量Sample sizen

实际观测的对象或独立观测数。 其他条件不变，均值标准误减半通常需要4倍样本量。

The number of observed units or independent observations. Other things equal, halving the SE of a mean usually requires four times the sample size.

The baseline claim assumed for calculating a test's reference distribution. Calculate a p-value in the reference world where H₀ holds.

The competing parameter claim evidence may support. Choose the direction from the question before inspecting the result.

显著性水平Significance levelα

检验预设的一类错误概率控制水平。 先定α，再比较p值；α不是假设为真的概率。

The preset Type I error control level for a test. Set α before comparing the p-value; it is not the probability a hypothesis is true.

原假设其实成立，却把它拒绝了。 把本来没有的差异判成有。

Rejecting a null hypothesis that is true. A false positive: declaring evidence of a difference when the null is true.

Failing to reject a false null hypothesis. β depends on the particular alternative; power is 1−β.

检验统计量Test statisticz, t

把样本结果与原假设的差异按标准误缩放。 一般结构：（估计值−原假设值）/原假设下的标准误。

Scales a sample's discrepancy from the null by its standard error. A common form is (estimate−null value)/SE under the null.

单样本均值检验 · One-sample mean tests

在H₀及模型条件成立时，得到至少同样极端结果的概率。 不是H₀成立的概率，也不表示效应大小。

Under H₀ and the model assumptions, the probability of a result at least as extreme as observed. It is neither the probability H₀ is true nor a measure of effect size.

同时关注高于与低于原假设值的偏离。 对称z检验的双侧p值包含两边尾部面积。

Tests for departures above or below the null value. For a symmetric z test, the two-sided p-value includes both tails.

检验总体比例是否符合指定值。 常用正态近似检验的SE按p₀计算，区间估计通常用p̂。

Tests whether a population proportion matches a specified value. The usual normal-approximation test uses p₀ in its SE; an interval usually uses p̂.
